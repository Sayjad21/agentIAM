Where to look

{# First, and wide: every other destination inspects a scenario something else already ran, and this is the only one that starts one. Mint because it is the one tile that does rather than shows. #}
RTRun a task→

Approve a task, set its ceiling, then delegate to sub-agents and watch the enforcement point allow and refuse. Start here.
ITIdentity tree→

The delegation graph for a task. Every sub-agent, its remaining budget, and the token blocks that narrowed it.
LDLive decisions→

Allow, deny and escalate as they happen. Each refusal names the caveat that caused it.
BLBudgets→

Spend against each mandate's ceiling, what is out on lease, and whether the ledger invariants hold.
PAPolicy→

Write a rule in English, compile it to Cedar, and see the corpus diff before it can be activated.
EQEscalations→

Requests waiting on a human. Approving narrows scope or amount; it can never widen them.
AXAudit→

Search the hash-chained ledger, trace a payment back to the human who approved it, and verify the chain.
