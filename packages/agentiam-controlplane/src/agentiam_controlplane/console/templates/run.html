Why this matters

A child token is minted by its parent, offline, and cannot exceed what the parent held — not by policy, by mathematics. Spawn one below with a smaller ceiling and then ask it to spend more than that: the refusal names the exact block that stopped it.

The root you create carries no attenuation block, so it declares no role. Cedar forbids a non-senior principal from touching payment_api, which is why the root itself is refused a payment — that is the organization's policy overruling a perfectly valid token.
